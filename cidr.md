### CIDR ranges

| Range           | First IP | Last IP        |
| ---             | ---      | ---            |
| **10.0.0.0/24** | 10.0.0.0 | 10.0.0.255     |
| **10.0.0.0/16** | 10.0.0.0 | 10.0.255.255   |
| **10.0.0.0/8**  | 10.0.0.0 | 10.255.255.255 |
| **0.0.0.0/0**   | (all)    | (all)          |

See: [CIDR notation](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc4632.html#section-3.1)
