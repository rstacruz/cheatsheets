## Hiding pages
<!-- {: .-snippets} -->

### Hide the sidebar link
<!-- {: .-snippet} -->

```yaml
---
title: Deprecated API
sidebar:
  hidden: true
---
```
<!-- {: data-highlight="hidden: true"} -->

**Keeps the page but drops its sidebar link.** Applies to autogenerated groups; an explicit `sidebar` entry still shows, and the URL still builds; see [sidebar frontmatter](https://starlight.astro.build/reference/frontmatter/#sidebar).

### Drop the page from builds
<!-- {: .-snippet} -->

```yaml
---
title: Next release
draft: true
---
```
<!-- {: data-highlight="draft: true"} -->

**Leaves the page out of builds.** It still renders in `dev` with a draft notice, and it is absent from the sidebar and search; see [draft](https://starlight.astro.build/reference/frontmatter/#draft).
